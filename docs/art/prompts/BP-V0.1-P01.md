Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: mobile game environment and gameplay art-direction validation image

Primary request:
Create one coherent 9:16 portrait gameplay scene for a cozy mobile idle/tycoon game, working title "Raccoon General Store." The scene is the first Art Concept Blueprint V0.1, but it must look like an actual playable game view rather than a presentation board, character sheet, collage, or infographic. Show a small late-Joseon-inspired Korean commoners' village that is beginning to recover from neglect. This is a warm simplified game world, not strict historical reconstruction.

Composition and camera:
Design a natural portrait-mobile composition without forcing fixed upper/middle/lower bands or predetermined left/right placement. Include one neglected but repairable small house and one restored generic Korean shop at the same visual scale, connected by a readable dirt path. Place one very small shopkeeper and two or three small animal customers at actual mobile gameplay scale; buildings must remain the dominant spatial units. Use top-down oblique projection: the ground is viewed from roughly 45 degrees above, while the important building facades and character bodies remain almost front-facing and readable. Use parallel projection. No vanishing point, no perspective convergence, no wide-angle distortion, no isometric diamond grid, and no 30-degree isometric tile layout. One unified continuous scene only.

Shopkeeper:
The shopkeeper is a Korean raccoon dog, Nyctereutes procyonoides, not a North American raccoon and not a red panda. Small rounded ears, warm gray-brown fur, only soft weak dark shading around the eyes, short legs, plump compact body, and one fluffy plain tail with absolutely no visible rings or stripes. Cute but not babyish; avoid a giant mascot head and oversized eyes. Simplified late-Joseon commoner work clothes only: short beige jeogori-like jacket, loose earth-brown trousers, and a small plain work apron. The face must stay simple enough to read at tiny gameplay scale.

Village and Korean identity:
Use modest hanok and thatched-house DNA: simple timber posts and beams, pale earthen walls, hanji lattice doors, restrained thatched or simple dark tiled roofs, low earth-and-stone wall fragments, and a tiny porch threshold. The abandoned building is weathered, dusty, slightly sagging, with a few missing roof pieces and dry grass, but still clearly repairable; no disaster ruin or violent destruction. The restored shop is tidy, structurally repaired, welcoming, and modest rather than wealthy or palatial. Show only a few everyday Korean props such as a small cluster of onggi or jangdok jars, one jige carrying frame, a woven basket, and at most one straw mat. Keep the entrance and path unobstructed. A few muted green plants can suggest revival.

Style and rendering:
Clean flat vector-like mobile game illustration. Consistent dark charcoal-brown outlines, flat color fills, almost no gradients, very subtle hanji paper grain, short simple contact shadows, and bold readable silhouettes at phone zoom-out. No cinematic lighting, no depth of field, no long dramatic shadows. Warm, humble, cozy, quietly hopeful.

Warm hanji beige #e8ddc8, muted straw #ddd0b6, charcoal brown #2b241b, roof gray #4a4a52, wood brown #8b6b4a, warm ochre #a8763e, muted leaf green #4a7c59, restrained red accent #a34a3a, dusty beige #d3c3a2. Warm neutrals dominate; red and green remain small accents.
